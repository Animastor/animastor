Animastor

Turn written stories into animated visual books — with audio, scenes, and a player.

Open App How to use

What is Animastor?

Animastor is a simple way to bring a text story to life. You give it a book (a plain .txt file), and it turns that text into a sequence of visual scenes with audio — something you can read, listen to, and watch like a short animated film of the story.

You don't need to be a designer, animator, or programmer. Open the app, upload your book, and watch it come alive scene by scene.

How to use Animastor

A simple workflow — five steps from a plain text file to a finished animated book.

  1. Open a book — upload your story as a plain .txt file inside the app.
  2. Generate — Animastor reads the text, splits it into scenes, and creates a visual version of the story.
  3. Listen & Watch — open the result as a visual book with audio: a scene at a time, like flipping through an animated picture book.
  4. Edit & regenerate — change the text, tweak a scene's visual style, or adjust audio. Regenerate only the parts you want to update.
  5. Export — when you are happy with the result, export the finished visual book.

Ready to try? Open the app and create an account — or sign in if you already have one.
