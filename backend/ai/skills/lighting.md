# Lighting

## Types
- **Natural**: Sunlight, moonlight, ambient. Soft, broad. Good for realism.
- **Hard**: Direct light, sharp shadows. Creates drama, tension, contrast.
- **Soft**: Diffused light, gentle shadows. Flattering, calm, romantic.
- **Backlight**: Light behind subject. Creates silhouette, separation from background.
- **Side light**: Light from side. Emphasizes texture, depth, creates mystery.
- **Top light**: Light from above. Harsh, interrogative, isolating.
- **Underlight**: Light from below. Sinister, unnatural, dramatic.

## Mood Through Lighting
- **Bright, even lighting**: Safe, happy, open, documentary.
- **Low key (mostly dark)**: Mystery, fear, intimacy, noir.
- **High contrast**: Drama, tension, conflict.
- **Color temperature**:
  - Warm (orange/yellow): sunset, fire, comfort, romance.
  - Cool (blue): night, cold, technology, sadness.
  - Mixed: conflict, unease, surreal.

## Practical Tips
- Use 3-point lighting for standard scenes: key light + fill light + back light.
- Motivate light sources from visible elements (lamps, windows, fire).
- Shadow length and direction indicate time of day.
