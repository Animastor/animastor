# Composition

## Rule of Thirds
Divide the frame into 3x3 grid. Place key elements along the lines or at intersections.

## Leading Lines
Use natural lines (roads, rivers, architecture) to guide the viewer's eye to the subject.

## Framing
Use foreground elements (windows, arches, trees) to frame the subject and add depth.

## Balance
- **Symmetrical**: Formal, stable, calm.
- **Asymmetrical**: Dynamic, interesting, modern.
- **Empty space**: Use negative space to emphasize isolation or focus.

## Depth
- Foreground: closest to camera.
- Midground: main action area.
- Background: environment/context.

## Color Composition
- **Complementary colors**: High contrast, vibrant.
- **Analogous colors**: Harmonious, calm.
- **Monochrome**: Focus on texture and form.
- **Warm colors**: Energy, passion, comfort.
- **Cool colors**: Calm, sadness, distance.
