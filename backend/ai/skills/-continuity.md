# Continuity

## Scene-to-Scene Continuity
- Maintain consistent character positioning between scenes.
- Track character emotional state across scenes.
- Ensure time of day flows logically between adjacent scenes.
- Weather and environment should change gradually unless a transition is specified.

## Within-Scene Continuity
- Unit-to-unit: character positions, lighting, camera angle should follow logically.
- Dialogue: responses must match the preceding line.
- Actions: if a character picks up an object, they should still have it in the next unit.

## Character Consistency
- Characters should act according to their established personality.
- Voice and speech patterns should be consistent.
- Relationships evolve gradually, not instantly.

## Audio-Visual Sync
- Audio and image should match the same moment in the scene.
- If audio describes rain, the image should show rain.
- Emotional tone of audio and visuals should align.

## Plot Continuity
- Track unresolved plot threads.
- Ensure cause and effect across scenes.
- Maintain timeline consistency (no paradoxes or skipped time without indicating it).
