# Prompt Engineering

## Image Prompt Structure
Format: `[Subject], [action/pose], [environment], [lighting], [camera angle], [style], [mood]`

Example: "A young woman in a flowing red dress, standing on a cliff edge, windswept moor at sunset, warm golden backlight with cool ambient fill, low angle, cinematic, dramatic and melancholic"

## Audio Prompt Structure
Format: `[Ambience], [foreground sounds], [emotional tone], [dynamics], [duration hint]`

Example: "Howling wind over open moor, distant thunder rumbles, a woman's faint humming, building tension, 15 seconds"

## Video Prompt Structure
Format: `[Scene description], [camera movement], [duration], [transitions], [overall mood]`

## Style Keywords
- **Cinematic**: dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, anamorphic, film grain.
- **Anime**: cel-shaded, vibrant colors, stylized, line art.
- **Watercolor**: soft, flowing, transparent, organic edges.
- **Black and white**: no color, high contrast, film noir.
- **Pixel art**: retro, low resolution, blocky, game-like.

## Negative Prompts
Specify what to avoid: "blurry, distorted, extra limbs, bad anatomy, watermark, text"

## Quality Boosters
- Add: "highly detailed, 8k, masterpiece, award-winning, professional"
- For audio: "high quality 44100Hz stereo, clear dialogue"

## Localization
- Prompts are typically written in English for best model results.
- Provide a Russian description alongside if needed for context.
